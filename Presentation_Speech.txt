[0:45 - 1:45] SLIDE 2: IDEA TITLE and PROPOSED SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
'SkillBridge provides an end-to-end, evidence-based translation mechanism between employer hiring signals and academic curricula.

It addresses the core problem through three innovative capabilities:
1. Automated AI Syllabus Auditing: Inspects course outlines in under 60 seconds, flagging obsolete topics like jQuery or legacy PHP, and suggesting ready-to-teach modern modules for TypeScript, Next.js, Docker, and Generative AI.
2. Supply-Demand Intelligence: Tracks live corporate vacancies against student enrollment to identify critical deficits vs. oversupplied tracks.
3. Interactive Capacity Simulator: Allows educational directors to model: What happens if we shift 500 seats from declining courses into Cloud DevOps? -- mathematically projecting the placement rate jump.'
